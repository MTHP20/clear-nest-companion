## 1. Who we are

ClearNest (the “**Service**”) is operated by **Pannon Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales. We provide a voice-first tool to help families organise practical and financial information through guided conversation with Clara, our AI voice companion.

For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, Pannon Ltd is the **data controller** in respect of personal data described in this policy.

---

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we process, why we process it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. It applies when you use our website, web app, Clara voice sessions, and related features.

**ClearNest is not legal, financial, or medical advice.** Nothing in this policy changes that. See our [Terms of Service](/terms).

---

## 3. Categories of personal data we process

Depending on how you use ClearNest, we may process:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Account and profile** | Name or nickname of the family organiser; name or identifier for the person Clara speaks with; age range; contact details you provide (e.g. email on waitlists). |
| **Voice and conversation** | Voice audio streamed for real-time conversation; transcripts of what is said; metadata such as session timestamps. |
| **Derived / structured data** | Information extracted from conversations (e.g. types of accounts, document locations, tasks) shown on your dashboard or in exports. |
| **Technical data** | IP address, device/browser type, and similar data in server or infrastructure logs needed to run and secure the Service. |
| **Special category data** | Conversations might **voluntarily** include health, care wishes, or other **special category** data under UK GDPR Article 9. We design Clara to minimise this and do not require health data to use the Service. |

We do **not** use your personal data for sale, advertising profiling, or unrelated marketing.

## 4. How and where data is stored

- **Encrypted transcripts and related server-side records** may be stored using **Supabase** infrastructure in the **European Union**, protected with **AES-256-GCM** (or equivalent industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest as configured).
- **Voice (raw audio)** is processed in real time by our voice AI provider; we aim to **delete raw audio within 24 hours** of capture, subject to technical and provider constraints. **Do not rely on the Service for emergency or clinical use.**
- **Local device storage**: Some information may also be held in your browser (e.g. localStorage) for session continuity and your dashboard experience until you clear it.

**We do not claim that no data ever leaves your device** — third-party processors (below) receive what they need to operate Clara and hosting.

## 6. Lawful bases

We rely on one or more of:

- **Consent** — where you clearly agree (e.g. ticking acceptance, continuing after a fair notice, or verbal consent captured at the start of a Clara session where applicable).
- **Contract** — processing necessary to provide the Service you requested.
- **Legitimate interests** — securing the Service, debugging, and improving reliability, balanced against your rights (e.g. minimal logging).

**Special category data** is processed only where the law allows — typically **explicit consent** or **substantial public interest** / scheduling conditions as applicable — and we minimise collection.

---

## 7. Retention

- **Raw voice audio**: targeted deletion within **24 hours** where technically feasible.
- **Transcripts and structured records**: retained for as long as needed to provide the Service and as described in-product, unless you ask us to delete them sooner (see below).
- **Local browser data**: until you clear site data or uninstall the browser.
- **Logs**: kept for a limited period for security and operations, then deleted or aggregated.

- **Access** your personal data  
- **Rectify** inaccurate data  
- **Erase** data (**“right to be forgotten”**) in certain cases  
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing  
- **Data portability** where applicable  
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (without affecting earlier lawful processing)  

To exercise these rights, contact **brownavi9@gmail.com**. We will respond within **one month** (extendable in complex cases as the law allows).

You may complain to the **ICO** (Information Commissioner’s Office): [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) · 0303 123 1113.
