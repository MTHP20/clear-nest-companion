## 1. Purpose

ClearNest interacts with older adults and potentially **vulnerable adults** through voice conversation. Clara is an **organisational tool**, not a care service or emergency service. Pannon Ltd nevertheless takes **safeguarding** seriously and expects everyone using ClearNest to do the same.

This policy explains our commitments, how Clara is instructed to behave, and what to do if someone may be at risk.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Clara (the ClearNest voice AI) and its system instructions  
- The ClearNest product and engineering team at Pannon Ltd  
- Anyone using ClearNest on behalf of a family  

---

## 3. Our commitments

1. **Respect autonomy** — Users can end a conversation at any time. Clara must not pressure someone to continue.  
2. **No capacity assessments** — ClearNest does **not** assess, record, or imply an assessment of **mental capacity**. Do not use ClearNest as evidence of capacity or lack of capacity.  
3. **Genuine consent** — Use ClearNest only when the person speaking **understands** what they are sharing and agrees **freely**, without coercion. If you are unsure, seek professional advice before continuing.  
4. **Respond to distress and risk** — Clara is instructed to acknowledge distress, offer to pause or stop, and **signpost** to appropriate help (including emergency services where relevant).  
5. **Not a substitute for statutory safeguarding** — We cannot replace Adult Social Care, the police, or clinical services.

---

## 4. Clara’s response protocol (summary)

These behaviours must be reflected in Clara’s system instructions:

### 4.1 Distress

- Acknowledge feelings without judgement  
- Offer to pause or stop  
- Do not push for organisational questions while the person is upset  

### 4.2 Abuse, neglect, or immediate danger

- Take disclosures seriously  
- Do not “investigate” or decide what happened  
- Encourage contact with **999** if there is immediate danger  
- Signpost to **Action on Elder Abuse** (see below) and local Adult Social Care where appropriate  

### 4.3 Medical emergency

- Direct the user to **999**  
- Do not continue with routine organisational topics until appropriate  

### 4.4 Suicidal thoughts or self-harm

- Respond with compassion  
- Signpost to **Samaritans** (116 123, 24/7)  
- Offer to end the conversation  
- Do not provide clinical assessment or “counselling”

---

## 6. Limitations

ClearNest **cannot**:

- Make formal safeguarding referrals on your behalf  
- Contact emergency services automatically  
- Monitor safety between sessions  
- Verify that someone is safe after a disclosure  

If you believe an adult is at risk, **contact the local authority** or **999** in an emergency.
