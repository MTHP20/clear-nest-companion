## 1. Purpose

ClearNest interacts with elderly and potentially vulnerable adults through voice conversation. While Clara is an organisational tool — not a care service — Pannonl Ltd recognises its responsibility to consider the safety and wellbeing of elderly users.

This policy sets out how ClearNest handles situations where a vulnerable adult may be at risk, distressed, or experiencing harm.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- Clara, the ClearNest AI voice agent
- The ClearNest product team at Pannonl Ltd
- Any third parties involved in delivering the ClearNest service

---

## 3. Our Commitments

Pannonl Ltd commits to:

1. **Respecting autonomy** — Clara will always respect the elderly user's right to end the conversation at any time, without question or pressure.

2. **Not assessing capacity** — ClearNest will never attempt to assess, record, or imply an assessment of the mental capacity of any user.

3. **Responding to distress** — If a user appears distressed during a conversation, Clara will acknowledge their feelings, offer to pause, and signpost appropriate support.

4. **Responding to disclosures** — If a user discloses abuse, mistreatment, or immediate danger, Clara will respond with care and provide emergency contact information.

5. **Not substituting for professional safeguarding** — ClearNest is not a care service and cannot initiate formal safeguarding referrals. Clara will always direct users to appropriate statutory services.

---

## 4. Clara's Response Protocol

### 4.1 If the user appears distressed or upset

Clara will:
- Acknowledge the user's feelings warmly and without judgement
- Offer to pause or stop the conversation
- Not continue to push for information
- Remind the user they can talk to someone they trust

Example response (to be included in Clara's system prompt):
> "It sounds like this might be a difficult topic. There's no rush at all — we can stop whenever you'd like. Is there someone in your family you'd like to be with right now?"

### 4.2 If the user discloses possible abuse, mistreatment, or neglect

Clara will:
- Take the disclosure seriously and respond with warmth and calm
- Not attempt to investigate or assess the situation
- Provide emergency contact information
- Clearly state that she is not able to take action herself, but that help is available

Example response (to be included in Clara's system prompt):
> "That sounds really serious and I'm glad you told me. I want you to know that help is available. If you're worried about your safety right now, please call 999. You can also call the Action on Elder Abuse helpline on 0808 808 8141 — it's free and confidential. Would you like me to pause our conversation so you can make that call?"

### 4.3 If the user mentions a medical emergency

Clara will:
- Direct the user to call 999 immediately
- Not continue the organisational conversation
- Offer to stop

### 4.4 If the user expresses suicidal thoughts or self-harm

Clara will:
- Respond with compassion and without panic
- Direct the user to the Samaritans (116 123, free, 24/7)
- Offer to stop the conversation
- Not continue asking about financial affairs

---

## 5. Clara's System Prompt Requirements

The following safeguarding principles must be reflected in Clara's system prompt at all times:

- Clara must stop the conversation immediately if the user says they want to stop, using any phrasing
- Clara must not ask about health conditions, diagnoses, or medical history
- Clara must not comment on the user's capacity or ability to remember
- Clara must respond to any disclosure of distress, danger, or abuse before continuing organisational topics
- Clara must include UK emergency contacts in her awareness and provide them when appropriate

**Emergency contacts to include:**
- Emergency services: 999
- Action on Elder Abuse helpline: 0808 808 8141 (free, confidential)
- Samaritans: 116 123 (free, 24/7)
- NHS non-emergency: 111

---

## 6. Limitations of ClearNest's Role

ClearNest is **not** a statutory safeguarding authority. We cannot:
- Make formal safeguarding referrals to Local Authority Adult Social Care
- Contact emergency services on behalf of a user
- Monitor user safety between sessions
- Verify whether a user is safe after a disclosure

If a family member (the Dashboard user) becomes aware through the app that an elderly relative may be at risk, they should contact their local Adult Social Care team or call 999 if there is immediate danger.

## 7. Internal Escalation (Pannonl Ltd)

If the ClearNest team becomes aware of a safeguarding concern — for example, through user feedback, a support request, or ElevenLabs conversation data — the following process applies:

1. The concern is logged by the receiving team member
2. The matter is escalated to the designated safeguarding lead at Pannonl Ltd: **[INSERT NAME AND CONTACT]**
3. The safeguarding lead assesses whether referral to statutory services is appropriate
4. If there is risk of immediate harm, emergency services are contacted without delay
5. All actions are documented

All Pannonl Ltd employees and contractors who interact with user data or product design must be made aware of this policy. Any team member with reason to believe a ClearNest user may be at risk must escalate immediately to the safeguarding lead.
