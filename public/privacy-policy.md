## 1. Who We Are

ClearNest is a product operated by Pannonl Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. We provide a voice-first organisational tool to help families prepare for later-life planning by capturing financial and practical information through guided conversation.

For the purposes of UK GDPR, Pannonl Ltd is the **data controller**.

---

## 2. What ClearNest Is — And Is Not

ClearNest is an organisational and planning tool. It is **not**:
- A legal service or source of legal advice
- A financial advisory service
- A medical device or clinical tool
- A regulated care service

Nothing ClearNest captures or displays constitutes legal, financial, or medical advice.

---

## 3. What Data We Process — And Where It Lives

### Data processed on your device only (browser localStorage)

ClearNest captures information from voice conversations and stores it **locally in your browser**. This includes:
- Financial account details (bank accounts, pensions, ISAs, investments)
- Property information
- Documents and legal references (wills, powers of attorney)
- Key contacts (GP, solicitor, accountant)
- Care wishes and preferences
- Action items and family notes

**Pannonl Ltd does not receive, store, or have access to this data.** It never leaves your device unless you explicitly download the PDF family report.

### Data processed by third-party services

When you use ClearNest, your voice and conversation data is processed by the following third parties:

| Processor | Purpose | Data processed |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| **ElevenLabs** (ElevenLabs Inc.) | Provides the Clara voice AI agent | Your voice audio and conversation transcript |
| **Anthropic** (Anthropic PBC) | Optional enhanced extraction from transcripts | Conversation transcript text (up to 4,000 characters) |
| **Vercel Inc.** | Hosts and serves the ClearNest web application | Server logs, IP address |

ElevenLabs stores conversation transcripts on their own infrastructure. Their retention and deletion policies are governed by their own Privacy Policy, available at elevenlabs.io. We have a Data Processing Agreement in place with ElevenLabs.

Anthropic processes transcript text for the purpose of extracting structured notes and action items. Their Privacy Policy is available at anthropic.com.

## 4. Special Category Data

Conversations with Clara may touch on health-related topics, end-of-life care wishes, or other sensitive personal information. Under UK GDPR, some of this may constitute **special category data** (Article 9).

ClearNest is designed to minimise collection of special category data. Clara's conversation is focused on practical and financial affairs and does not ask about health conditions, diagnoses, or medical history.

Any special category data mentioned voluntarily in conversation is:
- Processed solely by ElevenLabs and (optionally) Anthropic as above
- Not stored on Pannonl Ltd servers
- Stored locally on your device only if it appears in the conversation transcript extract

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

We rely on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR:

- **Contract performance** — processing necessary to provide the ClearNest service you have signed up to
- **Legitimate interests** — improving service reliability and detecting misuse (Vercel logs)
- **Consent** — where you have explicitly agreed to the processing of your data before beginning a session

## 6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
- **Erasure** — request deletion of personal data (note: as we hold no server-side data, this primarily applies to your local browser storage, which you can clear at any time)
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (the PDF export function serves this purpose)
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data we hold
- **Object** — object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** — you can stop using ClearNest at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **privacy@clearnest.co.uk**

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority:
**Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**
ico.org.uk | 0303 123 1113
