How it works

Three steps.
Everything covered.

Clara does the hard part. You get an organised dashboard ready to act on.

Step 1
You set it up
Takes two minutes. Add your parent's name, pick a time, and ClearNest does the rest. No technical knowledge needed.
Step 2
Clara calls your mum or dad
A warm, unhurried voice call — no apps, no smartphones required. Clara covers seven essential topics at their own pace.
Step 3
Dashboard updates live
Everything captured is organised instantly. Urgent gaps are flagged. A sharable Family Report is generated automatically.
Who it's for
