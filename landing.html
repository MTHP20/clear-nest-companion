How it works

Three steps.
Everything covered.

Clara does the hard part. You get an organised dashboard ready to act on.

1
You set it up
Takes two minutes. Add your parent's name, pick a time, and ClearNest does the rest. No technical knowledge needed.
2
Clara calls your mum or dad
A warm, unhurried voice call — no apps, no smartphones required. Clara covers seven essential topics at their own pace.
3
Dashboard updates live
Everything captured is organised instantly. Urgent gaps are flagged. A sharable Family Report is generated automatically.
Who it's for

Designed for two people who love each other.

The conversation is impossible between them. Clara makes it possible for both of them.

👴
The elderly parent
Gets a warm, patient voice call from Clara. No apps, no screens, no complexity. Just a gentle conversation at their own pace — and the comfort of knowing their family will be prepared.
👨‍💼
The adult child
Gets an organised dashboard, a Family Report they can take to a solicitor, and clear next steps — without having to have the conversation that neither of them can start.

The average Court of Protection case costs £20,000+ and takes 9 months. When no LPA is in place, families lose the ability to manage finances, make care decisions, or access a bank account — and the legal process to regain control is devastating. ClearNest flags missing LPAs before it's too late.
What Clara covers

Seven topics. One gentle conversation.

Clara works through everything your family needs — in order, at the right pace, with warmth throughout.

🏦
Bank Accounts
Which banks, where statements are kept, what accounts exist
💼
Pension Details
Provider, type, whether workplace or state pension
🏠
Property
Ownership, where deeds are kept, mortgage status
📄
Will & Documents
Whether a will exists, where it's kept, insurance documents
⚖️
Power of Attorney
Whether an LPA is in place, who is named, where stored
👤
Key Contacts

Privacy

Nothing leaves your family.

This is a promise, not a policy.

✓
Stored on your device only
ClearNest never sends your family's information to our servers. Ever.
✓
No account numbers
Clara is trained to ask where documents are kept — never for sensitive numbers.
✓
Consent every session
Clara always asks your parent's permission before each conversation begins.
✓
Family only access
You control who sees the dashboard. No third parties, no advertisers.
